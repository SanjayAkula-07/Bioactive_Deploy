Why XGBoost?

XGBoost has the best validation AUC* (0.759) and the best ROC-AUC on both validation (0.861) and holdout (0.861), and its CV and holdout results agree closely (accuracy 82.2% vs 81.8%), which indicates good generalisation. It balances sensitivity (0.901) and specificity (0.617); Random Forest and SVM reach higher sensitivity but at much lower specificity (0.293 and 0.433 on holdout), so they flag many inactive molecules as active.
